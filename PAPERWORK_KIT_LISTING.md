### Title
Contractor Estimate, Invoice & Change Order Templates — Fillable PDF Kit

### Description

Stop writing the same estimate and invoice structure from scratch every time. This kit gives you four ready-to-use, fillable PDF documents built specifically for contractors and home service businesses:

**What's included:**
- **Estimate Template** — itemized scope of work, pricing breakdown, and terms, ready to send to a customer
- **Invoice Template** — matching format for billing completed work, with due date and payment instructions
- **Change Order Form** — get it in writing (and get it signed) whenever a job grows beyond the original estimate, so scope creep doesn't turn into an argument later
- **Scope-of-Work Checklist** — a one-page reference of everything to include in an estimate so nothing gets left out or assumed

**How it works:** Open any of the PDFs in Adobe Acrobat Reader (free) or most modern browsers, click into any field, and type — no printing and handwriting required, though you can print and fill by hand too if you prefer.

**Who this is for:** Plumbers, electricians, flooring installers, roofers, HVAC techs, remodelers, and any home service business that's tired of formatting a new document every time.

**A note if you find yourself outgrowing paper templates:** if you're doing enough volume that typing up each estimate by hand feels slow, check out Bidoro — it uses AI to turn a customer's message directly into a scoped, priced estimate in about a minute. This kit works great on its own, or as a lighter-weight starting point before that.
